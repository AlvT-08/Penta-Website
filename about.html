Foundations and Early Growth

Penta Engineering Machineries, Inc. trace its roots back to October 1985, when it was established as a sole proprietorship under the name Penta Engineering and Machineries. Initially, the business focused on providing specialized service and repairs for pumps within the manufacturing and building trade sectors. By September 1989, the company transitioned into a corporation, officially becoming Penta Engineering Machineries, Inc.

Expanding Product Portfolio (1989-1996)

Following its incorporation, the company rapidly expanded its market presence through several key dealerships: 1989: Secured dealerships for PACO pumps, Fairbanks Morse fire pumps (now Fairbanks Nijhuis), and EIM submersible pumps. 1991-1992: Scaled up to exclusive distributorships for PACO pumps, EVAPCO evaporative cooling equipment, and EIM pumps. 1996: Added Amtrol tanks and Allen Bradley controls to its product offerings.

Modernization and Strategic Partnerships (2006-Present)

Entering the 21st century, Penta Engineering continued to diversify its technical solutions: 2006: Became the exclusive distributor of Tour & Andersson (now IMI TA) balancing valves and a dealer for Grundfos pumps. 2011: Added Nema expansion vessels and hydropneumatic tanks to its lineup. 2025: Most recently, the company expanded into heat transfer technology by becoming a dealer for Alfa Laval Gasketed Plate Heat Exchangers (GPHE).
